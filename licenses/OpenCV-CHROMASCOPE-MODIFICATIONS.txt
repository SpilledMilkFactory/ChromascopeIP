Linux builds exclude MagicYUV, CineForm and SANM before FFmpeg stream
probing and reject these codecs and non-video decoders again in the
selected playback context (CVE-2026-8461, CVE-2026-125268, CVE-2026-125266).
Audio decoding during probing is excluded. Other video codecs retain
the upstream behavior. Windows builds disable FFmpeg and are unmodified.

recorded-video capture (CVE-2026-125264, CVE-2026-125262, CVE-2026-125265).
